# Operator guide

1. Sign in with the assigned login/email. Mandatory initial password change is disabled. Voluntary changes are in Settings; new passwords must contain exactly four ASCII digits, including any leading zeroes, and require login again after saving.
2. Owner opens Settings to supply real shop address/contact details and creates accounts from private User management. No public signup exists.
3. Before ordinary opening, Owner may choose Settings → Start from previous accounts to enter an earlier period. Follow [the Bengali guide](historical-accounts-bn.md): starting Cash/Bank, held stock/old dues, confirm, chronological transactions, then reconciliation. Otherwise save ordinary opening cash/bank once, including zero/zero if appropriate. Opening adds equity, not revenue. Further capital uses Owner contribution; personal withdrawals stay separate from expenses. Neither path replaces/reset an already opened shop.
4. Add supplier/customer/person identities from Money & dues. Different people may share names; select the correct phone/ID. Do not merge by name.
5. Buy an exact unit: model/brand, Android/iPhone, storage, Android RAM, separate color/variant, condition, 15-digit phone IMEI, optional battery and box status; supplier, purchase cost, actual paid amount and account. Courier is paid immediately and either capitalized or expensed once. No planned selling price is required. Each accessory is one individually tracked unit without IMEI. Buy a phone from a person captures the seller's private identity/DOB and explicit with/without-box choice; permanent address is optional. Print its A4 declaration and obtain both handwritten signatures. An old sold/returned IMEI may begin a new acquisition cycle; two in-stock units cannot share an IMEI.
6. Select an in-stock unit when selling. Enter price, optional discount and actual cash/bank receipts; unused payment fields may stay blank. Credit requires a saved customer. Check the net bill, due and actual cost-based profit/loss. Below-cost sales are allowed. Review offers Save sale & open invoice or Save sale without printing; both save the same accounting effects and preserve later receipt access. The API supplies the canonical two-page A4 sales PDF only after a confirmed saved result.
7. Collect customer due/pay supplier using its original document. These change money/dues, not old sale revenue. Loans/advances have their own repayment workflows. Salary applies outstanding advance and expenses salary once; the remainder is paid. Duplicate active staff/month settlement is prevented.
8. Record expense with category, amount/account and optional details. A custom expense needs its custom name. Categories can be renamed/archived/restored without changing historical labels. An empty category list stays empty; add/restore a category explicitly.
9. Owner can select any valid daily date or inclusive From/To range, trailing seven days or current month, with per-model profit, expense and Cash/Bank statements and CSV. Staff can use only today's records/reports/exports; all stock costs and current operational balances remain visible. Today follows Asia/Dhaka. Historical-onboarding records retain their business date separately from the actual recording timestamp.
10. Count physical cash with Reconcile cash. A difference needs a reason. Counts never overwrite the ledger. Later linked corrections flag affected counts for review.

For a metadata mistake on unsold stock, use Edit stock details with a reason. For an accepted supplier return, use Return to supplier: stock is removed, remaining original payable is offset first, excess credit becomes supplier refund receivable, and unrecovered acquisition cost is expensed. Record actual refund receipts separately in Money & dues. An accepted supplier return cannot be generically reversed/deleted. For supported entry corrections, use Reverse a document with a reason; original records remain and dependent settlements must be reversed first. Sold/reacquired units and insufficient Cash/Bank can block a reversal. Customer returns/exchanges/warranty actions remain unapproved; ordinary backdating is unavailable outside the one-time historical setup.

If posting times out, keep the frozen submission and use Check submission status or retry the same submission. Do not create another entry with a new key. Closing the form preserves the draft. Network errors show cached reads; reconnect to post. Sign out after use on shared devices. Authenticated customer receipts omit internal cost/profit; no public receipt endpoint is introduced.
