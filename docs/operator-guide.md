# Operator guide

1. Sign in with the assigned login/email. On first use, change the initial password, then sign in again.
2. Owner opens Settings to supply real shop address/contact details and creates accounts from private User management. No public signup exists.
3. Owner saves opening cash/bank once, including zero/zero if appropriate. This adds equity, not revenue. Further capital uses Owner contribution; personal withdrawals stay separate from expenses.
4. Add supplier/customer/person identities from Money & dues. Different people may share names; select the correct phone/ID. Do not merge by name.
5. Buy an exact unit: model/brand/variant, condition, 15-digit phone IMEI, optional battery and box; supplier, purchase cost, paid amount and account. Courier is paid immediately and either capitalized or expensed once. No planned selling price is required. Each accessory is one individually tracked unit without IMEI.
6. Select an in-stock unit when selling. Enter price/discount and cash/bank receipts. Credit requires a customer. Check the net bill, due and actual cost-based profit/loss. Below-cost sales are allowed. Only a saved server result issues the final receipt.
7. Collect customer due/pay supplier using its original document. These change money/dues, not old sale revenue. Loans/advances have their own repayment workflows. Salary applies outstanding advance and expenses salary once; the remainder is paid. Duplicate active staff/month settlement is prevented.
8. Record expense with category, amount/account and details. Categories can be renamed/archived/restored without changing historical labels. An empty category list stays empty; add/restore a category explicitly.
9. Daybook selects a historical date for reading, with opening/inflow/outflow/closing per account, profit breakdown and export. Today follows Dhaka midnight and resume without discarding a draft. Reports offer Today, trailing seven days and Owner monthly profit.
10. Count physical cash with Reconcile cash. A difference needs a reason. Counts never overwrite the ledger. Later linked corrections flag affected counts for review.

For an entry mistake, use Reverse a document and supply a reason. Original records remain. Reverse dependent settlements before the original document. A sold purchase cannot be reversed until its sale correction restores the unit. A reversal can fail if its cash/bank effect would overdraw funds. These are error corrections, not a customer-return/refund policy. Formal returns and backdating remain unapproved.

If posting times out, keep the frozen submission and use Check submission status or retry the same submission. Do not create another entry with a new key. Closing the form preserves the draft. Network errors show cached reads; reconnect to post. Sign out after use on shared devices. Public receipts omit internal cost/profit.
